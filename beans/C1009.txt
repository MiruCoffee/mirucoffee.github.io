<!-- Producer Notes -->
<h4>Producer Notes</h4>
<div class="producer-notes">
  <p>
    Gikirima Factory is part of Kibugu Farmers Cooperative Society in Embu County,
    a celebrated coffee-growing region shaped by high elevation, volcanic soil,
    steady rainfall, and cool mountain temperatures. These conditions allow coffee
    cherries to ripen slowly, building the layered sweetness and lively structure
    that make Kenyan coffees so distinctive.
  </p>
  <p>
    The farmers supplying Gikirima primarily grow SL28 and SL34, two classic Kenyan
    varieties prized for their fruit character, clarity, and elegant acidity. After
    selective handpicking, the coffee is washed, density sorted, and dried on raised
    beds with careful attention throughout processing. The result is a polished cup
    with stone fruit sweetness, honeyed balance, and a clean black tea finish.
  </p>
</div>
