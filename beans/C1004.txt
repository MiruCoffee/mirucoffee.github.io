<!-- Producer Notes -->
<h4>Producer Notes</h4>
<div class="producer-notes">
  <p>
    This coffee comes from the Muhororo Washing Station in Rwanda's Nyamasheke
    region. Over 200 smallholders deliver cherries to the station, which is
    managed by Celestin Rumenenangabo and his family. The station operates with
    a strong emphasis on sustainability, transparency, and social impact.
  </p>
  <p>
    Cherries are carefully handled and dried on raised beds, helping preserve the
    coffee's red fruit, floral aromatics, and syrupy sweetness. The station also
    supports its local community through healthcare, education, livestock programs,
    and sustainability training.
  </p>
  <p>
    The cup reflects thoughtful processing and the care of the farming community
    behind it.
  </p>
</div>
