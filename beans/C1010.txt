<h4>Flavor Notes</h4>
<div class="bean-hero-flex">
  <div class="bean-text">
    <ul>
      <li>Tropical Fruit, Blackberry, Plum</li>
      <li>Honey, Grapefruit, Rose</li>
      <li>Organic Coffee Lot</li>
    </ul>
    <p>
      This Ethiopia Sidama natural comes from an organic coffee lot grown by
      approximately 600 smallholder farmers around the village of Bombe in Bensa,
      Sidama. Processed at Ayla Washing Station, it offers a vibrant,
      fruit-forward cup with tropical fruit, blackberry, plum, honey, grapefruit,
      and rose-like floral notes.
    </p>
    <p>
      This coffee uses an Improved Natural process: ripe cherries are sorted,
      floated to remove lower-density fruit, soaked for 12 hours, and then dried
      slowly on raised beds for about 28 days. That extra care helps build
      sweetness, clarity, and the honey-like character that makes this coffee
      feel lively and refined.
    </p>
  </div>

<!-- Producer Notes -->
<h4>Producer Notes</h4>
<div class="producer-notes">
  <p>
    Bombe is one of the most admired coffee-growing areas in Sidama, known for
    high elevation, careful cherry selection, and expressive fruit-forward cup
    profiles. This lot is processed at Ayla Washing Station near the Bonora River
    and the slopes of Durto Gerbamo Mountain, where cool mountain temperatures,
    rainfall, and rich soils help cherries mature slowly.
  </p>
  <p>
    In the cup, it brings the kind of expressive fruit, clean sweetness, and floral
    aroma that make Ethiopian natural coffees so memorable.
  </p>
</div>
