<h4>Producer Notes</h4>
<div class="producer-notes">
  <p>
    This Jauja coffee comes from El Pesadero and the Coto family in Antigua,
    Guatemala.
  </p>
  <p>
    Antigua is surrounded by three volcanoes: Volcan de Agua, Volcan de Acatenango,
    and Volcan de Fuego. Rich volcanic soil, warm days, cool nights, and steady
    winds create the region's distinctive microclimate, giving this coffee its
    layered sweetness and clean structure.
  </p>
</div>
